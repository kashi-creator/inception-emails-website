How it works & pricing

Pricing is scoped to your market and volume, and quoted on the call. Month-to-month, and you keep the assets.

How it works

  1. List built and verified. We find companies that look like your best clients: seat count, vertical and drive time from your office. Every contact is verified.
  2. Separate domains, warmed. We set up sending domains and inboxes that aren't yours, configure SPF, DKIM and DMARC, and warm them for several weeks.
  3. Emails you approve. We write short, plain emails. Nothing goes out until you've said yes.
  4. Meetings on your calendar. We handle replies and book qualified meetings with decision-makers on your calendar.

What's included

  • A target list built around the clients you already like serving, with every contact verified.
  • Separate sending domains and inboxes, so your primary domain and your M365 tenant never send.
  • SPF, DKIM and DMARC on every sending domain, several weeks of warmup, and sending capped at about 20 emails per inbox per day.
  • Emails written for you, sent only after you've approved them.
  • Reply handling: we answer, deal with the "not now"s and unsubscribes, and forward anything only you can answer.
  • Qualified meetings with decision-makers booked on your calendar.
  • A weekly reply log showing every reply.

Pricing

Pricing is scoped to your market and volume, and quoted on the call. It depends on how many companies in your area fit your ideal client and how much sending it takes to reach them. On the call you'll see the 50 companies we'd target first, the emails we'd send, and what each held meeting would cost you.

Month-to-month

No annual contract. Stay while it's producing qualified meetings; stop when it isn't.

You keep the assets

If you leave, the sending domains, the verified lists and the email copy are yours. You don't start from zero.

Founding MSP client rate

We're offering a founding rate to a limited number of early MSP clients. Ask about it on the call.

Compared with hiring

A full-time BDR costs $60–100k a year in salary before benefits, tools and ramp time, and someone still has to manage them. That someone is usually you.
